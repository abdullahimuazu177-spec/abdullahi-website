Helpful information

Frequently asked questions.

Quick answers to common questions about document support.

How do I request a service?

Contact Abdullahi by email or WhatsApp and describe the document you need, your goals, and any relevant deadline. You can discuss the scope before deciding whether to proceed.

Can you improve an existing resume or CV?

Yes. Resume editing and strengthening can focus on clarity, organization, formatting, wording, and presenting your actual experience more effectively.

Can you help with LinkedIn?

LinkedIn profile optimization can include your headline, About section, experience descriptions, and skills, aligned with your professional goals.

Do you guarantee interviews or employment?

No. Professional documents can help communicate your qualifications, but hiring decisions depend on many factors. No interview, job, or business outcome can be guaranteed.

How do you handle my information?

Share only information needed for your project. Before sending sensitive personal information, discuss how it will be used and handled. Do not send passwords or account login codes.

Can I ask a question before ordering?

Yes. Use the contact options on this page to ask a question and describe what you would like help with.
